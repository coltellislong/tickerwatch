Green rows are buy signals · hover or tap “Why?” for the plain-English reason · click a row to open it on Yahoo Finance
Prices from Yahoo Finance, listings from Nasdaq, financial statements from SEC filings. Long-term score (0–100) = financial quality 25 + growth 20 + valuation 20 + competitive strength 15 + risk 10 + trend 10. Metrics without free data (analyst forecasts, moat and governance reviews) are left out and the rest re-weighted. Buy review = score ≥ 85, valuation ≥ 14/20, price ≥ 20% below the DCF fair value, no red flags. Watchlist = score ≥ 75. Risk review = a red flag such as weak interest coverage, heavy dilution or shrinking revenue. Momentum score = average percentile rank of 1M, 3M, 6M and 1Y returns (0–100). Signals: Breakout = uptrend (price above its 50-day average, which is above its 200-day), within 3% of its 1-year high, RSI 55–75, trading 1.2× normal volume. Buy the dip = growth score ≥ 80 and long-term uptrend, down 2–10% this week, RSI 35–50. Too hot = RSI ≥ 80 or up 60%+ in a month. Only stocks priced ≥ $5 trading ≥ $5M a day qualify. For viewing only, not investment advice.
