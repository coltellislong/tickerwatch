Green rows are buy signals · hover or tap “Why?” for the plain-English reason · click a row for the full detail view
Prices from Yahoo Finance, listings from Nasdaq, financial statements from SEC filings. Long-term score (0–100) = financial quality 25 + growth 20 + valuation 20 + competitive strength 15 + risk 10 + trend 10. Metrics without free data (analyst forecasts, moat and governance reviews) are left out and the rest re-weighted. Buy review = score ≥ 85, valuation ≥ 14/20, price ≥ 20% below the DCF fair value, no red flags. Watchlist = score ≥ 75. Risk review = a red flag such as weak interest coverage, heavy dilution or shrinking revenue. Momentum score = average percentile rank of 1M, 3M, 6M and 1Y returns (0–100). Signals: Breakout = uptrend (price above its 50-day average, which is above its 200-day), within 3% of its 1-year high, RSI 55–75, trading 1.2× normal volume. Buy the dip = growth score ≥ 80 and long-term uptrend, down 2–10% this week, RSI 35–50. Too hot = RSI ≥ 80 or up 60%+ in a month. Only stocks priced ≥ $5 trading ≥ $5M a day qualify. 1-year score = quality 30% + value 25% + momentum 25% + trend 10% + risk 10%. 1-yr buy = score ≥ 75, quality ≥ 60, momentum ≥ 50, price above its 200-day average, not overheated, no red flags; hold about 12 months, exit below the 200-day average or −15%. My portfolio signals: Accumulate = owned, score ≥ 80, still attractively valued, under the 5% position and 25% sector caps; Trim review = position over 5% or price 60%+ above fair value; Exit review = a red flag or score below 50. Portfolio Fit (0–100) = room under the position cap 40 + room under the sector cap 35 + diversification 25. For research only, not investment advice.
